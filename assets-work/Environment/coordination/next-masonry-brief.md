## First study: HouseEtc01 front-face sculpture only

Use current baseline50tri (26 dragon,24 block) and rebuild the CENTRAL dragon face as deliberately connected carved forms. Preserve the complete outer block skin, top/bottom contact planes, all outer perimeter vertices/edges and a continuous unmodified border around the decorated front. Do not bevel any block cap or outer side. Actual stack30/31 is the decisive contact proof: even a5unit chamfer introduced a visible V-notch despite unchanged bounds and most anchors.

Useful intervention: three broad depth groups following the painting—brow bridge above recessed eye sockets, projecting muzzle/nasal ridge, and swept cheek/frill planes separated from a recessed background bed. Keep original nose maximum and inherited bounding envelope; obtain relief through cutting the bed behind the motif rather than enlarging module projection. Use purposeful edge loops around the painted features and continuous lips at socket/cheek transitions. No subdivided uniform triangle grid, no procedural dimple field. Make the relief readable first with a neutral-clay diagnostic and the frozen textured reduced image before duplicating it.

Why earlier work failed: rejected-study/relief.py uniformly subdivided every dragon triangle5ways and applied seven narrow UV ellipses with4–6unit strength. It spent most geometry on internal texture detail without reorganizing broad face planes. Its paired mouldings recessed generic rectangular panel beds5units but did not improve the dominant motif. A larger numeric displacement alone is not the solution: intentional broad sculptural surfaces and meaningful negative spaces are required.

Protect the outer border as surface triangles, not merely a set of corner coordinates. Inward relief must also avoid cutting through thin regions. The shared c_wall06 UV layout is a registration guide, not authorization to change every consumer. Textures remain frozen.
